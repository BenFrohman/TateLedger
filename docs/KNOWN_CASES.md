# Known cases (literature, not this repo)

Author: Benjamin Stanley Frohman
Copyright (c) 2026 Benjamin Stanley Frohman
License: Apache-2.0

- Divisors on abelian varieties over finite fields (Tate 1966):
  Hom(A, B) is the Galois invariants in Hom(T_ell A, T_ell B).
- K3 surfaces over finite fields (Charles, Madapusi Pera, Maulik, Kim, and others):
  Tate for divisors, including Artin's conjecture that supersingular K3s
  have Picard number 22.
- Codimension-2 cycles on cubic fourfolds over finite fields of char p >= 5
  (Charles, via the associated holomorphic symplectic variety).
  That is Tate on a cubic in characteristic p, not Hodge on a complex sextic.
- Many elliptic surfaces (Artin-Swinnerton-Dyer and later).
- Abelian varieties in low dimension: Tate is treated in the literature
  through dimension 5 over finite fields. Record the paper; do not copy
  it here as an original theorem of this ledger.

None of these is a constructor for an unspecified fourfold over an
unspecified finite field.
