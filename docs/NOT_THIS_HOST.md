# The locked complex host does not set Tate up

Author: Benjamin Stanley Frohman
Copyright (c) 2026 Benjamin Stanley Frohman
License: Apache-2.0

The three-chain sextic V(F) subset P^5_C is a complex variety.
Its Jacobian ring, Griffiths numbers, Gram bound rho >= 3, and
Orlov equivalence D^b ≃ HMF^{gr}(F) are complex / derived data.

They do not produce:

- a reduction of V(F) modulo p with a named Galois-invariant class,
- a proof that that class is algebraic over F_q,
- an eigenvalue identity that constructs a cycle.

An eigenvalue q^2 is a footprint.
A 4-form is a class.
Neither is a surface.

Until someone writes Z_i with cl(z) = gamma on a named variety over a
named finite field, the arithmetic sentence stays open.
