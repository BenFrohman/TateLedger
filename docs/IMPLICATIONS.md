# Implications if Tate is true

Author: Benjamin Stanley Frohman
Copyright (c) 2026 Benjamin Stanley Frohman
License: Apache-2.0

These are consequences recorded in the literature. They are not theorems of this repository.

## Motives over finite fields

A strong form of Tate would describe Grothendieck motives over F_q:
every motive is a Tate twist of a summand of an abelian variety tensored
with an Artin motive. Semi-simplicity of Galois on etale cohomology
travels with that package.

## Zeta poles and BSD

For a surface over F_q, Tate for divisors is equivalent to: the Picard
number equals the order of the pole of zeta(X, s) at s = 1. For an
elliptic surface that is equivalent to BSD over the function field of the
base.

## Brauer groups

Tate for divisors on a surface over a finite field is equivalent to
finiteness of the Brauer group, and to finiteness of the Tate-Shafarevich
group of the Jacobian of a pencil.

## Standard conjectures in characteristic p

Tate plus comparison of ranks gives numerical = homological equivalence
over finite fields (conjecture D in that setting).

## Passage through Hodge

If Hodge holds over C and Tate holds over finite fields, comparison
theorems let you recognize a class in one world and transport a cycle to
the other. Hodge for CM abelian varieties is known to imply Tate and the
standard conjectures for abelian varieties over finite fields. That is a
one-way bridge, not a constructor on an unnamed complex fourfold.
