Patches are welcome when they keep the ledger honest.

1. Do not prove Tate by renaming. A definition `Tate := True` is rejected.
2. Do not write sorry in a released theorem. An open sentence stays a Prop.
3. A cycle is a subvariety (or a finite Q-combination of them). An eigenvalue, a 4-form, a matrix factorization, and a derived equivalence are not cycles.
4. Name the variety and the class. Unspecified X and unspecified gamma are the open sentence, not a constructor.
5. Keep Tate and Hodge in separate columns. A complex host does not automatically give a reduction modulo p.
6. Cite the literature when you record a known case.
7. Every new file carries `Copyright (c) 2026 Benjamin Stanley Frohman` and Apache-2.0.
8. Pull requests against main. Describe the mathematical change in one paragraph.

## Closed on sight

- Set the statement equal to True.
- The eigenvalue q^2 is the cycle.
- Orlov / HMF / a (2,2)-form on V(F) proves Tate.
